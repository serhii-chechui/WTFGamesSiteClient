Privacy Policy — Ludorama

Effective date: 2026-07-26

Ludorama is a mobile board-game app for iOS and Android, published by WTFGames ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Ludorama collects, why, and what choices you have.

Plain-language summary

  • Ludorama does not require you to sign up with an email, password, or real name. When you first launch the app, we create an anonymous account for you.
  • We generate a random device ID (not your phone's advertising ID) to recognize your account across launches, and we store your chosen in-game nickname, your level/experience, and your in-game (virtual) currency balance on our server so your progress is saved.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous authentication, basic usage analytics, and crash reporting, so we can keep the app working and fix bugs.
  • The app asks for an age check before you play. Players under 13 can still play, but in a restricted, offline mode: no account is created, no device ID or profile data is sent to our servers, no nickname/level/currency is stored, and Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics do not run. We never store your birth date on our servers — only an age category.
  • You can export a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from inside the app.
  • Ludorama does not currently offer any real-money purchases — only virtual, in-game currency. If real-money in-app purchases are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the payment data involved.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

5. Children's privacy and the age gate

Ludorama includes an in-app age check ("age gate") that runs before anything about you leaves the device, and players under 13 are welcome to play — in a restricted, offline mode described below.

How the age gate works

  • The age gate asks for your birth date on-device only, the very first time you open the app. Your birth date itself is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Based on your birth date, the app determines an age category — "13 or older" or "under 13" — and it is this category (not your birth date) that is sent to our backend and stored against your account, if you have one.
  • If you are under 13, the app locally records the date you will turn 13; nothing else about the check is stored. Once that date arrives, the restriction is automatically lifted and the app re-evaluates normally. Otherwise, your answer is stored once on your device and you are not asked again.

What is different if you are under 13

If the age gate determines you are under 13, the app runs in a restricted, account-free, offline-only mode. Concretely, based on the app's actual behavior:

  • No account is created. The app does not generate/send a device ID to our backend and does not call our guest-authentication endpoint, so no player account exists on our servers for you.
  • No player profile data is collected. Because there is no account, no nickname, level, experience, or in-game currency balance is collected, stored, or synced for under-13 players.
  • Firebase does not run. Firebase (anonymous authentication, Analytics, and Crashlytics) is never initialized for under-13 players, so no usage analytics and no crash/diagnostic data is collected from the app in this mode.
  • No ad-tracking prompt. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt is not shown and no tracking authorization is requested in this mode.
  • You can still play. The core board-game experience remains fully playable offline; you simply won't have cloud-saved progress, a nickname, or a persistent level/currency balance while under 13.
  • Data export / deletion: since no server-side account or data exists for under-13 players, the in-app export and delete features (Section 7) have nothing to act on in this mode — there's nothing on our servers to export or delete.

This is intended to satisfy the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and, in the EU/UK, the GDPR provisions on children's data (sometimes called "GDPR-K"), by simply not collecting personal data from children in the first place, rather than collecting it under parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us in a way that is not permitted by this policy, please contact us at serhii.chechui@gmail.com so we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete the data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the rights described below. We honor these rights regardless of location where reasonably possible.

In-app self-service (available to everyone)

  • Export your data — from your player profile in the app, you can request a copy of the data we hold about your account. This calls our GET /api/v1/users/me/export endpoint and returns your data.
  • Delete your account — from your player profile in the app, you can permanently delete your account and its associated data. This calls our DELETE /api/v1/users/me endpoint. This action cannot be undone.

GDPR rights (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") — available in-app via account deletion, or by contacting us.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — available in-app via data export.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

CCPA / CPRA rights (California)

If the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Request deletion of your personal information — available in-app via account deletion, or by contacting us.
  • Request a copy of your personal information — available in-app via data export.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell or "share" (as defined by the CPRA) personal information, so there is no opt-out of sale/sharing to request.

Exercising your rights

You can exercise the export and deletion rights above directly in the app, from your player profile. For any other request, or if you have trouble using the in-app tools, contact us at serhii.chechui@gmail.com.
