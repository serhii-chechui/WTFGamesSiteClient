Ludorama — Account and Data Deletion

App: Ludorama
Developer: ФОП Чечуй Сергій Васильович (Serhii Chechui, Individual Entrepreneur / ФОП), trading as WTFGames

This page explains how to delete your Ludorama account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account (in-app)

Ludorama creates an anonymous guest account for you automatically; you never sign in with a username, email, or password. You can permanently delete that account and its associated data directly inside the app:

  1. Open Ludorama on your device.
  2. Go to your Player Profile (tap your profile/avatar on the main menu).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and its server-side data are then permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.

Deletion by request (alternative)

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email at serhii.chechui@gmail.com. Please send the request from your device using the in-app support option where possible, or include enough information for us to identify the account. Because accounts are anonymous and tied to a device identifier, we may ask you to send the request from the affected device so we can locate the correct account.

What is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove the data associated with it, including:

  • Your device-linked guest account and authentication record.
  • Your player nickname (playerName).
  • Your game progression stored on our servers: experience, level, and in-game virtual currency/wallet balance.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: residual copies of data may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten.
  • Analytics/diagnostics: aggregated or anonymized analytics and crash-diagnostic data that is not linked to your account may be retained by our analytics/crash providers (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics) according to their retention settings.
  • Legal/fraud: we may retain limited records where required to comply with legal obligations or to prevent fraud/abuse.

For full details on what data we process and why, see our Privacy Policy.
