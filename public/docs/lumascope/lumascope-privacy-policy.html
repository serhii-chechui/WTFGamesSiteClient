Privacy Policy — LumaScope

Effective 1 October 2026

LumaScope collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network access whatsoever: the application and its Quick Look extension are sandboxed and built without the network entitlement, so they cannot connect to the internet even in principle. Every photometric file you open, every chart and report you export stays on your Mac.

This privacy policy applies to LumaScope, a macOS application for viewing, charting and comparing IES (LM-63) and EULUMDAT (LDT) photometric files, together with the Quick Look extension it installs, developed by Serhii Chechui (WTFGames).

Serhii Chechui ("DEVELOPER") has adopted this privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") to explain what information LumaScope does and does not handle, and how the application behaves on your computer.

Should you have any questions relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ghost201087@gmail.com

1. Data We Collect

None. DEVELOPER does not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any data from your use of LumaScope. Specifically, the application does not collect:

  • personal data of any kind — no name, e-mail address, account or registration is required or requested;
  • usage or analytics data — there is no analytics SDK and no telemetry endpoint;
  • device, advertising or tracking identifiers;
  • location data, contacts, or access to the camera or microphone;
  • the contents of the photometric files you open, or of the charts, reports and tables you export.

LumaScope displays no advertising, contains no advertising technology and performs no tracking, in the sense of the App Tracking Transparency framework or otherwise. It makes no network requests at all, including update checks.

2. What the Application Stores on Your Mac

LumaScope keeps a small amount of information on your own computer so that it works the way you left it. This information never leaves your Mac and is never accessible to DEVELOPER:

  • Recently opened files and folders — up to ten, kept for File → Open Recent as macOS security-scoped bookmarks in the application's own preferences, so that an item reopens without you having to grant access to it again. File → Open Recent → Clear Menu removes the list.
  • Your exports — charts (PNG, PDF, SVG), PDF reports and CSV tables, written only to the locations you choose in a save dialog. Copying a chart puts it on your own clipboard.
  • Diagnostics — when something goes wrong, for example a file that cannot be read, LumaScope may write a message to the standard macOS system log (Apple's unified logging). These messages stay on your Mac, are managed by macOS, are subject to its default privacy redaction and are never sent to DEVELOPER or anyone else.

3. Files and Folders

LumaScope runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It can read only the .ies and .ldt files and folders you explicitly hand to it — in the Open panel, by dragging them onto the window or the Dock icon, or by opening them from Finder — and write only where you choose in a save dialog. It does not scan your disk, index your files or access anything you have not handed to it.

The Quick Look extension reads only the file you preview in Finder (with the Space bar or in the preview pane), draws its chart on your Mac and keeps nothing. It has no access to any other file.

Your files are never uploaded, copied off your device, or shared with DEVELOPER or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services and Components

LumaScope integrates no third-party services or SDKs: no analytics provider, no advertising network, no crash reporting service, no cloud storage, no social features. It is built only on Apple's own system frameworks.

5. Apple and the Mac App Store

LumaScope is distributed through the Mac App Store. The purchase and download are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by DEVELOPER. DEVELOPER can see only the aggregated, anonymous sales and usage reports that App Store Connect provides, which do not identify individual users and are not combined with any other information.

6. Support Correspondence

If you write to us for support, we necessarily hold your e-mail address and whatever you chose to include in your message — for example a description of a problem, or a photometric file you attached. This is used solely to answer you. It is not used for marketing, is not shared with anyone and is deleted once the matter is resolved and there is no longer a reason to keep it.

7. Data Retention

As DEVELOPER collects no data through the application, there is nothing for DEVELOPER to retain. The information described in section 2 stays on your computer and is under your control: the recent items list is removed with Clear Menu or when the application is deleted, exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them yourself, and system log messages are managed and expired by macOS. Support correspondence is retained as described in section 6.

8. Your Rights

Data-protection law, including the GDPR, gives you rights of access to, correction of, portability of and erasure of your personal data. Because DEVELOPER neither collects nor processes personal data through LumaScope, there is no such data to give you access to, correct, export or erase. If you have written to us for support, you may ask us at any time to delete that correspondence, using the contact address below.

9. Children

LumaScope is a professional lighting tool suitable for all ages and rated accordingly. It collects no information from anyone, children under 13 included, and contains no advertising, no purchases and no user-to-user communication.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

DEVELOPER reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at reasonable times — for instance if a future version of the application gains a feature that changes how it handles information. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised, so please review it occasionally. Your continued use of the application will signify your acceptance of the changes to this Privacy Policy.
