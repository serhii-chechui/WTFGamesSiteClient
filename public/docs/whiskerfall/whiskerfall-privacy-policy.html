Privacy Policy — Whiskerfall

This privacy policy applies to the Whiskerfall game for iOS and Android devices, together with any related services (collectively, the "Application"), developed by Serhii Chechui (WTFGames), hereby referred to as the "Service Provider". The Service Provider acts as the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to collect as little data as possible. When you download and use the Application, the following information may be processed:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those screens
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system and device model you use
  • Gameplay and usage analytics collected with GameAnalytics, as described in the "Analytics" section below
  • Information about in-app purchases, as described in the "In-App Purchases" section below

The Application does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, physical address, phone number, photos, or precise location.

Analytics

The Application uses GameAnalytics, a game analytics service provided by GameAnalytics ApS, to understand how players use the Application, find and fix problems, and balance and improve the game. GameAnalytics processes this data on behalf of the Service Provider. The following information may be sent to GameAnalytics:

  • Identifiers: a random user identifier generated by the Application and a vendor-specific device identifier (such as the iOS identifier for vendor). Advertising identifiers (the iOS IDFA or the Google Advertising ID) are used only where your device settings allow it and, on iOS, only if you grant permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Device and technical data: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, Application version, SDK version, connection type, and language. Your approximate country is derived from your IP address.
  • Session data: session start and end time, session length, and number of sessions.
  • Gameplay events: progression (for example, starting, finishing, or failing a run), in-game resources earned and spent, and design events describing how game features are used.
  • Purchase events: the item purchased, its price and currency, and the store transaction details needed to validate the purchase.
  • Error events: error messages and technical details about crashes and malfunctions.

Analytics data is used only in aggregated form to improve the Application. It is not used to identify you personally, to build advertising profiles, or to serve targeted advertising, and it is not sold. GameAnalytics processes the data under its own privacy policy. To request access to or deletion of the analytics data related to your device, contact the Service Provider at serhii.chechui@gmail.com.

In-App Purchases

The Application is free to download and offers optional in-app purchases of virtual items and other digital content. In-app purchases are processed entirely by the store you downloaded the Application from: the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

  • Payment details. Your payment card number, billing address, and other payment information are collected and processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. The Service Provider never receives or stores them.
  • Transaction information. To deliver and restore your purchases, the Application receives from the store, and may process, the product identifier, transaction or order identifier, purchase date and time, purchase status, the store receipt or purchase token, and the price and currency of the item. This information does not include your name or contact details.
  • Purpose. Transaction information is used only to verify that a purchase is valid, deliver the purchased content, restore purchases on a new or reinstalled device, prevent fraud, handle support requests, and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Purchase validation. Purchases are handled through Unity In-App Purchasing, which may process transaction information on behalf of the Service Provider to validate receipts.
  • Refunds and cancellations. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies. Requests should be made through the store where the purchase was made.
  • Controlling purchases. You can disable or restrict in-app purchases in your device settings (Screen Time on iOS, purchase authentication and parental controls in Google Play).

Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, the Service Provider processes personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — to process, deliver, and restore in-app purchases you make;
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Application and to prevent purchase fraud, in a manner that does not override your rights and freedoms;
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for analytics with GameAnalytics and any other non-essential processing, where consent is required by law;
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law, including tax and accounting requirements for purchases.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the Application, contact the Service Provider at serhii.chechui@gmail.com and that information will be deleted promptly.
