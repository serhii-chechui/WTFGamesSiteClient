Privacy Policy — Bakeneko

Effective 17 September 2026

Bakeneko collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network access whatsoever: the app is sandboxed and is built without the network entitlement, so it cannot connect to the internet even in principle. Every model you import, every map you bake and everything you paint stays on your Mac.

This privacy policy applies to Bakeneko, a macOS application for baking and painting PBR texture maps, developed by Serhii Chechui (WTFGames).

Serhii Chechui ("DEVELOPER") has adopted this privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") to explain what information Bakeneko does and does not handle, and how the application behaves on your computer.

Should you have any questions relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ghost201087@gmail.com

1. Data We Collect

None. DEVELOPER does not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any data from your use of Bakeneko. Specifically, the application does not collect:

  • personal data of any kind — no name, e-mail address, account or registration is required or requested;
  • usage or analytics data — there is no analytics SDK and no telemetry endpoint;
  • device, advertising or tracking identifiers;
  • location data;
  • the contents of the models, textures or projects you work on.

Bakeneko displays no advertising, contains no advertising technology and performs no tracking, in the sense of the App Tracking Transparency framework or otherwise. It makes no network requests at all, including update checks.

2. What the Application Stores on Your Mac

Bakeneko keeps a small amount of information on your own computer so that it works the way you left it. This information never leaves your Mac and is never accessible to DEVELOPER:

  • Preferences — new-project defaults, graphics-tablet pressure settings and similar options, stored in the application's own preferences.
  • Recently opened projects — stored as macOS security-scoped bookmarks, so that a project reopens without you having to grant access to the same folder again.
  • Your work — project packages (.pbrproj) and exported texture maps, written only to the locations you choose in a save or export dialog.
  • Diagnostics — if macOS produces a crash or hang report for Bakeneko through MetricKit (Apple's own on-device diagnostics API, whose reports carry no device or user identifier), the application writes that report into its own sandbox container, keeping at most the twenty most recent ones. These files are never uploaded, never sent to DEVELOPER and are not shared with any third party. Deleting the application removes them along with everything else in its container.

3. Files and Folders

Bakeneko runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It can read and write only the files and folders you explicitly select in an open, save or export dialog, plus its own container. It does not scan your disk, index your files or access anything you have not handed to it.

4. Third-Party Services and Components

Bakeneko integrates no third-party services: no analytics provider, no advertising network, no crash reporting service, no cloud storage, no social features. The application bundles one third-party source component — ufbx, an MIT-licensed FBX reader — which runs entirely on your Mac as part of the application and has no network access of its own.

5. Apple and the Mac App Store

If you obtain Bakeneko from the Mac App Store, the purchase and download are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by DEVELOPER. DEVELOPER can see only the aggregated, anonymous sales and usage reports that App Store Connect provides, which do not identify individual users and are not combined with any other information.

6. Support Correspondence

If you write to us for support, we necessarily hold your e-mail address and whatever you chose to include in your message — for example a description of a problem, or a file you attached. This is used solely to answer you. It is not used for marketing, is not shared with anyone and is deleted once the matter is resolved and there is no longer a reason to keep it.

7. Data Retention

As DEVELOPER collects no data through the application, there is nothing for DEVELOPER to retain. The information described in section 2 stays on your computer for as long as you keep the application installed, and is under your control: preferences and diagnostics are removed when the application is deleted, while projects and exported maps remain wherever you saved them until you delete them yourself. Support correspondence is retained as described in section 6.

8. Your Rights

Data-protection law, including the GDPR, gives you rights of access to, correction of, portability of and erasure of your personal data. Because DEVELOPER neither collects nor processes personal data through Bakeneko, there is no such data to give you access to, correct, export or erase. If you have written to us for support, you may ask us at any time to delete that correspondence, using the contact address below.

9. Children

Bakeneko is a professional graphics tool suitable for all ages and rated accordingly. It collects no information from anyone, children included, and contains no advertising, no purchases and no user-to-user communication.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

DEVELOPER reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at reasonable times — for instance if a future version of the application gains a feature that changes how it handles information. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised, so please review it occasionally. Your continued use of the application will signify your acceptance of the changes to this Privacy Policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or about the application itself, are welcome at ghost201087@gmail.com. The Bakeneko user manual covers how the application works.
